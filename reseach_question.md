# Research Question

## 1. Research Topic

Gene Expression Analysis in Lung Adenocarcinoma.

## 2. Main Research Question

How do gene expression patterns differ between
lung tumour tissues and non-tumour lung tissues?

## 3. Background

Gene expression is the process by which information
from genes is used by cells to perform biological
functions.

Changes in gene expression may be associated with
different biological conditions and diseases.

Lung adenocarcinoma is a type of lung cancer.

The selected dataset GSE10072 contains gene expression
information from lung tumour and non-tumour tissues.

## 4. Research Objectives

1. Understand the concept of gene expression.
2. Study the selected public biological dataset.
3. Understand differences between tumour and
   non-tumour tissue samples.
4. Learn the importance of biological datasets
   in bioinformatics.

## 5. Expected Learning Outcomes

- Understand basic gene expression concepts.
- Learn how to identify biological datasets.
- Understand the use of public datasets.
- Develop basic bioinformatics knowledge.

## 6. Dataset Reference

NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)

Dataset ID: GSE10072

https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE10072
